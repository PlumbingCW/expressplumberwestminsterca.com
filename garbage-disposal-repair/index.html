Emergency: a disposal that leaks, smokes or floods the cabinet

.

A disposal that is dripping onto the cabinet floor or filling the sink with water is a problem to handle right now. Power and water do not mix, and the disposal is wired to a switch and a plug or a hard connection nearby.

Turn off the wall switch, then the breaker. Put a bowl or pan under the leak. Run no water into the sink until it has been looked at. If water is spraying, close the angle stops under the sink.

Jammed or humming: the allen key fix and the reset button

.

A disposal that hums but does not spin is jammed. Most units have a hex socket in the center of the underside. Turn the power off, insert the allen key and work it back and forth until the flywheel moves freely.

A dead disposal with no sound has usually tripped its thermal overload. Press the red reset button on the underside after the motor cools for a few minutes. If it trips repeatedly, the motor is struggling and needs a plumber.

Finding the leak: flange, hose or the unit itself

.

Dry the unit, wipe it down and run water with a few paper towels underneath. The towel shows where the drip starts. A leak at the top comes from the sink flange, where the putty or gasket has dried out.

A leak where the drain pipe or dishwasher hose meets the side is often just a loose clamp. A leak from the bottom or seams of the body means the housing is cracked, and it needs to be replaced. No repair holds on a cracked housing.

How Westminster's hard water shortens a disposal's life

.

Westminster water averages 252 mg/L hardness, roughly 14.7 grains per gallon. Scale builds up on the grinding chamber, the drain outlet and the seals. A disposal can slow down, smell stale or leak earlier than expected.

Cold water while grinding helps move food waste. Ice cubes and citrus peel give a rinse, but they do not remove scale. Mineral deposits build quickly in a hard water kitchen, and a water softener reduces that load on every appliance.

What goes down the disposal, and what never should

.

A disposal handles soft food scraps with plenty of cold water. It does not handle grease, coffee grounds in bulk, pasta and rice, potato peels, fibrous vegetables or bones. These settle in the trap and turn into a clog further down.

Skip chemical drain cleaners. They sit in the unit, damage seals and leave a hazard for the next person to open the drain. Grease poured down any kitchen drain also hardens in the sewer line, which is a problem for the Midway City Sanitary District's system as well as your own pipes.

Air gaps, dishwasher hookups and why the sink backs up

.

The dishwasher drains through the disposal in most kitchens. A knockout plug left in the dishwasher inlet during an install is a common reason a new unit leaks or the dishwasher will not drain.

Some kitchens use an air gap fitting on the sink deck or a high loop in the hose to stop dirty water siphoning back. If water spits from that fitting, the disposal or the drain line downstream is blocked. Standing water in a double sink usually means a clog beyond the disposal.

Water in the other sink bowl.

If the second basin fills when the disposal runs, the drain line past the unit is partly blocked. A new disposal will not fix this. The line needs cleaning.

Repair or replace: how to decide

.

Repair is worth it for a jam, a loose connection or a worn flange gasket. Replace the unit if the housing is cracked, the motor trips constantly, or the disposal is noisy and rusted. Matching horsepower to the household is part of the choice.

Garbage disposal questions from Westminster homeowners

.
Do you handle garbage disposal repair emergencies at night or on weekends in Westminster?

Yes. The phone line is answered 24/7, including nights, weekends and holidays. Call, describe what the disposal is doing, and switch it off at the breaker while you wait.

Why is my disposal humming but not spinning?

It is jammed. Turn off the power, insert a hex key in the socket on the underside and turn it back and forth until it moves. Then press the reset button and test it with water running.

Where is the reset button on a disposal?

It is a small red button on the bottom of the unit. Wait a few minutes for the motor to cool before you press it. If it trips again right away, call a plumber.

Why is my disposal leaking?

The leak comes from the top flange, the side hose connections or a cracked body. Look at where the water starts to drip. A cracked housing means the disposal has to be replaced.

Can I put chemical drain cleaner down the disposal?

No. It sits in the chamber, damages the seals and puts anyone working on the sink at risk. Use hot water, a plunger or call for drain cleaning.

How long should a garbage disposal last?

It depends on use and water quality. Hard water scale and grease shorten it. A unit that leaks from the body or trips constantly is near the end.

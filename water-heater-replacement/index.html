When a tank starts leaking, the fastest way back to hot water is a straight swap of the old heater for a correctly sized new one.

A replacement is more than lifting one tank out and another in. The size, venting, strapping, drain pan and relief piping all matter. If your old heater is still working and you are planning an upgrade, water heater installation covers the planned side of it.

Emergency tank leak: stop the water first

.

A tank that is leaking from the body cannot be repaired. Water will keep escaping until the supply is closed, so the cold shutoff comes first. If it is gas and you smell gas, leave the house, do not use switches, call from outside or a neighbor's home, and call 911 if anyone is in danger. SoCalGas, if that is your provider.

A dripping fitting or relief valve is a different story and may be a water heater repair. If you are not sure, call and describe where the water is coming from.

Gas or electric: what changes the swap

.

Staying with the same fuel is the quickest swap. Gas units need a vent that draws properly and a gas connection that is tight and tested. Electric units need a circuit that matches the heater's wattage and a clear space to work.

Switching fuel is possible but is closer to an installation, with new gas piping or a new circuit. Heat pump and hybrid heaters also exist. They need space, a condensate drain and a warm-air allowance, so they suit some garages and not tight closets.

Choosing the tank size for your household

.

Tank size follows the number of people and how many hot fixtures run at once. Bigger is not automatically better. An oversized tank costs more to buy and to keep hot.

These are general guides. A plumber will compare the first-hour rating to your real usage before you decide.

Seismic strapping, relief piping and the expansion tank

.

California Health and Safety Code section 19211 says new and replacement water heaters must be braced, anchored or strapped. Strapping matters because a loose tank in an earthquake can tear off its gas or water lines.

A temperature and pressure relief valve needs a discharge pipe that runs to a safe spot. If your home has a closed system with a backflow device or pressure regulator, an expansion tank may also be needed to absorb the thermal expansion.

What roughly 14.7 grains of hardness means for the new tank

.

Westminster's 2024 water quality report shows average total hardness of 252 mg/L, roughly 14.7 grains per gallon. Scale builds on the bottom of the tank and on electric elements, and it shortens the life of any heater that is never flushed.

A new heater is a good time to start a yearly flush habit. If you want to cut the scale at the source, a softener or filter is worth asking about.

Water heater replacement questions from Westminster homeowners

.
Do you handle water heater replacement emergencies at night or on weekends in Westminster?

Yes. The phone line is answered 24/7, including nights, weekends and holidays. Call and describe the leak, and we will arrange a visit, with same-day appointments offered when available.

How long does a water heater last?

Many tank heaters last somewhere around eight to twelve years, depending on water quality, flushing and how hard they work. Hard water usually shortens that.

Is the old heater removed?

Ask what is included when you call, including haul-away of the old unit.

Can I move to a different fuel type?

Often yes, but it may require new gas piping or a new electrical circuit. Talk it through on the phone and we will explain what is involved.

Do I need a drain pan?

A pan under a tank in a finished space or closet helps catch leaks and direct water to a drain. Ask when you call.

What does a replacement cost?

It depends on the size, fuel type, venting and access. A typical tank replacement can run from a little over a thousand dollars to several thousand. You get a clear price before work starts.

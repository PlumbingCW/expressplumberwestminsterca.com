How to check any plumber's license

.

You should verify any plumber you hire, and California makes that easy through the Contractors State License Board.

Visit the CSLB website, choose Check a License, and search by the license number or the business name. Ask any contractor for a license number before work starts, and confirm that the license is active.

What we work on

.

Our services fall into five groups: leaks and pipes, drains and sewer lines, water heaters, fixtures, and installs and upgrades. The full services page links to each one with symptoms and options.

Emergency work includes burst pipes, sewer backups and failed water heaters. Planned work includes repiping and tankless water heater service.

What we do not do

.

This site does not cover gas line repair. If you smell gas, leave the building first, call from outside or a neighbor's home, and contact the gas utility. SoCalGas, if it is your provider, lists its emergency number as 1-800-427-2200.

Prices on this site are general industry ranges, not our quotes. Jobs vary by access, parts and building age, so your price comes from a conversation about your specific problem.

Why local conditions matter to us

.

Westminster's housing is mostly from the 1960s boom, with a median build year near 1971. That means aging copper, galvanized lines and cast iron drains in many homes.

The city's water averages about 252 mg/L hardness, which leaves scale on heaters and fixtures. We plan repairs with that wear in mind, because a part that fails from scale will fail again if the cause is ignored.

Permits and inspections.

Some plumbing work may need a permit. Ask the City Building Division at (714) 548-3254 whether yours does.

Repair or replace: how we frame the decision

.

A repair makes sense when the failed part is cheap and the rest of the system is sound. Replacement makes sense when the same system keeps failing or the cost of repeated repairs approaches the price of new.

Water heaters, galvanized lines and old disposals follow that pattern in Westminster homes. We lay out both options and the likely cost of each, so the choice stays yours.

Standards and local sources.

Plumbing work in California follows the California Plumbing Code (Title 24, Part 5), and contractor licenses are listed by the Contractors State License Board.

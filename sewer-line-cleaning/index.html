Sewer line cleaning clears the main pipe that carries all household waste out to the street. It is a bigger job than a sink clog, and the right approach depends on what is blocking the line and where.

Sewer backup emergency: first steps right now

.

Raw sewage carries bacteria, so treat any backup as a health matter. Keep children and pets away, avoid touching standing water and wear gloves and boots if you must move items.

Do not run the washing machine, take a shower or flush. Open windows if the smell is strong, and call so we can start planning the clearing before we arrive.

How to tell it is the main line and not one drain

.

A single slow sink is local. A main line problem has a different pattern.

  • More than one fixture gurgles or drains slowly
    • .
  • A toilet flush makes the tub or floor drain bubble
    • .
  • Water backs up in the lowest tub, shower or floor drain
    • .
  • Sewer smell appears near drains or in the yard
    • .

If you see this pattern, a sink cable will not help. The blockage sits farther down, past where branch lines join.

Finding your cleanout before the plumber does

.

Most homes have a capped cleanout, a pipe stub with a threaded cap, near the foundation or along the side of the house. It gives direct access to the main line without pulling a toilet.

If you cannot find one, tell us when you call. Many Westminster homes were built in the 1960s and early 1970s, and a cleanout may be buried, painted over or missing entirely, which changes how we reach the line.

What clogs a main sewer line in Westminster

.

Grease, wipes, hair and paper build up over time, and tree roots enter at joints in older clay and cast iron pipe. A line from the original tract era can hold small gaps where roots find moisture.

A belly, a low sagging section, collects solids and causes repeat clogs even without roots. If the line has backed up before, we may suggest a camera inspection after clearing to see what happened.

Machine clearing versus jetting on the main

.

A sewer machine is a heavy cable with cutting heads, sized for four-inch lines. It works well on roots and dense blockages, and it clears most sudden backups.

When grease or scale coats the pipe, hydro jetting cleans the wall and keeps the line open longer. We pick based on what is in the pipe, not on which tool is newest.

Cutting down repeat backups

.

Flush only toilet paper, and keep grease, wipes and paper towels out of the line. Even products labeled flushable tend to hold together.

If roots are the cause, cleaning is a temporary fix. Ask about sewer line repair if backups keep returning, since removing the entry point is what ends the cycle.

Sewer line cleaning questions from Westminster homeowners

.
Do you handle sewer line cleaning emergencies at night or on weekends in Westminster?

Yes. The phone line is answered 24/7, including nights, weekends and holidays. Call and tell us what is backing up, and we will go through the next steps.

How do I know if my sewer main is clogged?

Several drains gurgling at once, or a floor drain backing up when you flush, usually means the main. A single slow sink is a branch clog.

Can I use a drain snake on the main line myself?

A small hand snake does not reach far or cut well in a four-inch line. It can also push a clog farther in. A proper machine and a known access point work better.

Who is responsible for the sewer line, me or the district?

That varies by where the property line responsibility starts. Ask Midway City Sanitary District or a plumber to confirm it before assuming either way.

What does sewer line cleaning cost?

It depends on access, line length and what is blocking it. Typical industry costs run from a few hundred dollars to more for heavy jobs. We give a clear price before work starts.

Will the clog come back after cleaning?

It can if roots, a belly or grease coating remain. A camera look after clearing shows why the line clogged and what would prevent a repeat.

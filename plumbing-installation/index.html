Jobs we install: fixtures, remodels and reconnections

.

We also reconnect sinks and toilets after countertops, flooring or cabinets are replaced. If you are working with another contractor, we can come in for the plumbing portion only.

Planning the layout before the walls come down

.

The cheapest plumbing move is the one that stays put. Moving a toilet or tub drain means opening floors or slab, while swapping a fixture in the same spot is simple.

Decide where fixtures go before drywall, tile or cabinets are ordered. Sending us the fixture model numbers lets us check rough-in dimensions, drain size and supply needs. This avoids the delay of discovering a mismatch on installation day.

Old homes, new fixtures: what we find in 1970s Westminster houses

.

The median Westminster home was built around 1971, so many remodels expose original pipe. Corroded stops, brittle supply lines and mismatched fittings are common once the cabinet is emptied.

That means a new fixture sometimes needs new valves, supply lines or a short section of pipe to go in properly. If the old supply system is failing across the house, a whole-house repipe can be done before the new fixtures go in.

Hard water and the fixtures you pick

.

City of Westminster water averages 252 mg/L hardness, roughly 14.7 grains per gallon. Scale builds on spouts, shower heads, cartridges and heating elements.

Fixtures with smooth finishes and removable aerators are easier to keep clean. A softener or filter installed with the remodel protects everything you install. Water heaters and tankless units are especially sensitive, so we plan for those when relevant.

Choose fixtures you can service.

Pick faucets with standard cartridges and shower valves that can be replaced from the front. Easier service means lower repair costs later when scale takes its toll.

Permits and inspections: ask the city first

.

Whether a permit applies depends on the work. The City Building Division at 8200 Westminster Blvd, phone (714) 548-3254, can tell you. Their permit worksheet lists items such as water heaters, fixtures, backflow devices and building sewer work.

Our advice is to ask before you start, not after. Unpermitted work can cause delays at sale time and gaps in inspection. Walk-ins are accepted on weekdays during business hours.

Gas lines and what falls outside plumbing installation

.

We do not do gas line work on this page. Gas piping for ranges, dryers and heaters is handled by a gas fitter. If you smell gas, leave the house, do not use switches, call from outside or a neighbor's home, call 911 if anyone is in danger, and call SoCalGas at 1-800-427-2200 if SoCalGas is your gas provider. Call 911 if anyone is in danger, and do not touch light switches.

Drain and sewer problems are also separate jobs. A clogged line or backup is handled under drain cleaning. Sewer work in Westminster is tied to the Midway City Sanitary District, and a plumber can help confirm where property line responsibility starts.

What affects the price of an installation

.

Installation costs vary a great deal. Simple fixture swaps can be modest, while remodels that move drains or open walls cost more. Material, access, and the condition of the existing shutoffs and pipes drive most of the difference.

We give a clear price before work starts, so you can compare it against your remodel budget. Ask about bundling several fixtures into one visit, since setup time is shared.

Standards and local sources.

New plumbing follows the California Plumbing Code (Title 24, Part 5), and the City Building Division, (714) 548-3254, lists fixtures and backflow devices on its permit worksheet. Verify any contractor's license on the Contractors State License Board website.

Plumbing installation questions from Westminster homeowners

.
What kinds of fixtures do you install?

We install faucets, toilets, sinks, vanities, tub and shower valves, disposals, dishwasher connections and outdoor spigots. We also reconnect fixtures after remodeling work.

Do I need to buy the fixture before you arrive?

Yes, it helps. Having the fixture on site lets us check the rough-in and fit before the work starts. Send the model number ahead of time so we can spot any issues.

Do I need a permit for a plumbing installation in Westminster?

It depends on the work. Ask the City Building Division at (714) 548-3254 whether a permit applies to your project. They can tell you what is required.

Can you move a toilet or tub in my remodel?

Yes, in many cases. Moving drains costs more, since it can involve opening the floor. We review the layout and explain the options before work begins.

Do you install gas lines?

No. Gas piping is handled by a gas fitter. If you smell gas, leave and call SoCalGas at 1-800-427-2200 if they are your provider.

Will new fixtures work with my old pipes?

Usually, but old stops and supply lines may need replacing. We check these on site and tell you before we proceed.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. You can verify any contractor license on the CSLB website under Check a License.

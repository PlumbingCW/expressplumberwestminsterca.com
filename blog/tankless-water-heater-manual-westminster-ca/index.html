How to Read Your Tankless Water Heater Manual in Westminster, CA

.

A tankless heater manual is long, but only six sections decide whether the unit runs well for years.

Written by Westminster Express Plumbing. Published October 24, 2026. 6 minute read.

Most owners open the manual once, then lose it. That is a mistake, because the manual lists the error codes, the flush schedule, the venting limits and the gas and water requirements for your exact model. If you do not have it, the model number on the unit's label usually leads to a free download from the maker.

This guide walks through what to look for, in the order that matters. It does not name brands, since layouts differ, but nearly every manual covers the same ground. Start with the maintenance pages, because hard water is the biggest threat to a tankless water heater in this area.

Westminster note

Westminster tap water averages 252 mg/L of total hardness, roughly 14.7 grains per gallon, and readings range up to 413. Scale forms on the heat exchanger and shrinks the passages that water flows through. Check the manual's flush section and plan on an annual descaling, or sooner if you see temperature swings or error codes.

Find the model number and the right manual first

.

The rating label on the side or bottom of the heater shows the model and serial number. Write both down and keep a photo on your phone. Manuals often cover several models, so make sure the tables you read match your number.

If the installer left a packet, look for the quick-start card, the warranty page and any installer notes. Do not rely on a manual for a different model, since gas pressure, vent size and flow rates change from one to the next.

Error codes: the table you will use most

.

Nearly every manual has an error code chart with a number, a meaning and a suggested action. Codes are grouped by cause: ignition, flame, temperature sensors, venting, water flow and scale. Look up your code before you restart anything.

Some codes clear with a simple reset. Others warn of a safety shutdown, so do not keep resetting a unit that fails again. Note the code and the time, then decide whether the fix is yours or a plumber's.

Flush and descale instructions

.

Look for a section called maintenance, descaling or flushing. It lists the valves to close, the hoses to attach and the amount of descaling solution or white vinegar to circulate. The usual time is 45 minutes to an hour with a small pump.

Isolation valves make this easy. Without them, a plumber must add valves first. If your unit lacks them, ask about installing them before the first flush.

Do not skip the inlet screen.

Grit collects on the small filter at the cold water inlet. A clogged screen cuts flow and can trigger low flow codes that look like a heater failure.

Venting, clearances and condensate

.

The venting chapter lists approved pipe types, maximum lengths, the number of elbows allowed and the clearance to windows and openings. Never change the vent without reading it. A bad vent can let exhaust gases back into the home.

Condensing models also drain acidic condensate. The manual shows where the drain goes and whether a neutralizer cartridge is needed. Check that the drain line stays clear and slopes properly.

Flow rate, temperature settings and why showers go cold

.

Every tankless unit has a maximum flow rate in gallons per minute at a certain temperature rise. In cold months the incoming water is colder, so the unit gives fewer gallons at the same hot temperature. The manual's chart shows this trade-off.

If two showers and a dishwasher run at once, the unit may hit its limit and the water cools. This is a sizing issue, not a defect. Lowering the set temperature a few degrees often stretches the flow.

Gas and water requirements worth double-checking

.

The installation pages list gas input in BTU, the gas line size and the minimum gas pressure. A unit that fires poorly on a busy day may have a line that is too small. That needs a plumber or gas fitter, not a manual reset.

If you ever smell gas, leave the house and call from outside. If SoCalGas is your gas provider, the emergency line is 1-800-427-2200. Call 911 if anyone is in danger, and do not touch light switches.

Gas smell means leave first.

Do not troubleshoot a gas smell with the manual. Get out, call from outside or from a neighbor's phone, and wait for the utility to clear the home.

Maintenance schedule you can paste on the wall

.

Most manuals recommend annual professional service and more frequent owner checks. Put the schedule on a note near the unit so the next person in the house knows what to do.

Standards and local sources.

Follow the maker's manual first, because installation and service rules come from it and from the California Plumbing Code (Title 24, Part 5). According to the City of Westminster's 2024 Water Quality Report, hardness averages 252 mg/L, so the flush schedule matters.

Tankless water heater manual questions

.
Where can I find my tankless water heater manual?

Look for the model number on the unit's rating label, then search the maker's support page for that exact model. Save a copy on your phone.

How often should I flush a tankless heater in Westminster?

Westminster water averages roughly 14.7 grains per gallon, so annual flushing is a sensible starting point. Follow your manual if it asks for more often.

What should I do when my tankless heater shows an error code?

Look up the code in the manual, note it, and try only the listed reset. If it returns, or the code is about venting or gas, stop and call a plumber.

Why does my tankless heater give lukewarm water?

Common reasons include scale, a clogged inlet screen, or too many fixtures running. Check the flow rate chart in the manual.

Can I change the vent pipe myself?

No. The manual limits vent type, length and elbows for safety. Have a licensed plumber do vent changes.

Does the manual cover gas line size?

Yes, the installation section lists the gas input and the line size needed. If your unit starves for gas at peak use, the line may be too small.

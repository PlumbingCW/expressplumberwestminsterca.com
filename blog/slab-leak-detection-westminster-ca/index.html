Slab Leak Detection in Westminster, CA: Signs, Methods and Next Steps

.

A slab leak is a water line leaking under or inside your concrete floor, and finding the exact spot before anyone cuts concrete saves time and money.

Written by Westminster Express Plumbing. Published October 24, 2026. 6 minute read.

Slab leak detection means locating a leaking pipe under a concrete foundation without breaking up more floor than necessary. The usual warning signs are a warm spot on the floor, the sound of running water when everything is off, a spinning water meter and a higher bill.

You can confirm a leak yourself with a simple meter test, and a plumber then pinpoints it using listening equipment, thermal imaging or a pressure test. This guide covers each sign, the test, the methods and the repair choices.

Westminster note

About 55 percent of Westminster homes are detached single-family houses and about 10 percent are mobile homes, so the layout under your floor may be a slab, a raised floor or a pier foundation. Check which you have, because a slab leak test and repair only apply to concrete floors, while a raised floor allows access from below.

Signs that point to a slab leak

.

A slab leak rarely announces itself with a puddle. Water often travels through the soil under the slab, so the signs show up indirectly.

One sign alone can have another cause, but two or three together make a slab leak likely.

Common slab leak signs

.
  • A floor spot that feels warm, especially over a hot water line
    • .
  • The sound of running or hissing water when all fixtures are off
    • .
  • A water meter that moves with every tap closed
    • .
  • A bill that jumps with no change in use
    • .
  • Damp carpet, lifting laminate or cracked tile
    • .
  • Low hot water pressure or a water heater that never stops running
    • .

Confirm it with a meter test first

.

A meter test shows whether water is leaking somewhere on your property. It is free and takes about fifteen minutes.

  1. 1

    Stop all water use

    .

    Close every faucet, make sure the dishwasher and washing machine are idle, and pause any ice maker or irrigation.

    2. .
  2. 2

    Read the meter

    .

    Open the meter box and note the dial or leak indicator and the digits.

    3. .
  3. 3

    Wait fifteen minutes

    .

    Use no water at all during this time.

    4. .
  4. 4

    Read it again

    .

    If the numbers or the indicator moved, water is going somewhere. A moving meter points to a leak, though not necessarily under the slab.

    5. .

To narrow the location, shut off the main valve at the house and watch the meter again. If it stops, the leak is inside the house side. If it keeps moving, the leak is between the meter and the house.

How plumbers actually find the leak

.

Good detection uses more than one method, since each has limits. Concrete, flooring and soil all change what instruments can read.

Why pressure testing matters

.

A pressure test separates the hot line from the cold line and each leg of the plumbing. The plumber caps a section, applies pressure and watches the gauge.

That narrows the search to one pipe run, so listening equipment is only needed over a short stretch of floor. It also keeps the repair from guessing, which matters when the next step involves cutting concrete.

Do not ignore a warm floor.

A warm spot on the floor over a hot line is one of the clearest signs of a slab leak. Turn off the water heater and the main valve if the floor is hot or wet, and call a plumber.

What to do while you wait for a plumber

.

Protect the house first. Water under a slab can lift flooring and encourage mold.

Shut off the main valve if water is reaching the surface or the meter is running fast. If the leak is slow, you can leave the water on but limit use. Photograph damaged flooring for your insurance record.

Move rugs, furniture legs and boxes off wet areas, and run a fan if the floor feels damp. Do not drill or cut into the slab yourself, since supply lines, electrical conduit and gas lines may run through the same area.

Repair options once the leak is located

.

The method depends on where the pipe runs and how much of the system is worn. A single failed spot is very different from a line that is failing in many places.

Why older Westminster slab homes are worth watching

.

Westminster housing has a median year built of about 1971, and many homes of that era sit on a concrete slab with copper supply pipes running underneath. Copper develops pinholes with age, and a pinhole under a slab can leak for months before the signs show.

If you have had one slab leak, ask about the condition of the rest of the line. Where several spots are failing, a repiping plan may cost less than repeated repairs.

Hard water, pressure and the water meter

.

Westminster water averages roughly 14.7 grains per gallon of hardness, and pH is 7.9. Mineral buildup inside older pipes can contribute to wear and restrict flow, so a low-pressure complaint is not always a leak.

To check the meter or a suspected leak on the city side, call the Water Division at (714) 894-3796, Monday to Friday, 7:30 to 4:30. After hours, water emergencies go to (714) 898-3315. If the problem sits between the meter and the house, water line repair may be the right service.

Slab leak questions from Westminster homeowners

.
How do I know if I have a slab leak in Westminster?

Look for warm floor spots, running-water sounds with fixtures off and a moving water meter. Do the fifteen minute meter test, then call a plumber to confirm the location.

Can a slab leak be found without breaking the concrete?

Yes. Acoustic, thermal and pressure methods locate most leaks from the surface. Concrete is only cut once the spot is confirmed.

Is a slab leak an emergency?

Treat it as urgent. Shut off the main valve if water is surfacing, and call a plumber, since constant leaking can damage the foundation area and flooring.

How long does slab leak detection take?

Many detection visits take a couple of hours, depending on the number of lines tested and the flooring.

What does slab leak detection cost?

It varies with the number of tests and the access. Detection often runs a few hundred dollars, and repair can reach several thousand depending on the option. A clear price should come before work starts.

Who do I call if the leak is on the city side of the meter?

Call the City Water Division at (714) 894-3796 during business hours. Confirm where the property line responsibility starts before paying for repairs.

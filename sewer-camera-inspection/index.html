Sewer Camera Inspection in Westminster, CA

.

A camera on a flexible cable shows exactly what is wrong inside a sewer line, so repairs are based on evidence and not guesses.

Call (714) 750-8637.See all services.

A sewer camera inspection feeds a small waterproof camera into your drain or main line and records the pipe from the inside. You see cracks, roots, bellies and buildup on screen, along with the distance to each problem.

It is a planned service, but our 24/7 line is open if a backup cannot wait. Most inspections answer one question: why does this line keep giving trouble, or is it sound enough to buy?

What the camera can and cannot see

.

The camera shows pipe material, joints, cracks, offsets, root growth, grease, scale and low spots holding water. A locator at the surface can mark where the camera sits, so we can pinpoint a problem from above.

It cannot see through a full blockage. If the line is backed up, we clear it first, then run the camera to see what caused the clog.

Before you buy a Westminster home

.

A standard home inspection looks at the house, not the pipe under the yard. Westminster's median home dates to about 1971, so the lateral may be clay or cast iron that is now more than half a century old.

A camera look before closing shows if the line has roots, cracks or a belly. That gives you a repair estimate to bring into negotiation, instead of a backup after you move in.

Ask for the footage.

Request the recording and the written findings. If a seller or agent offers a past inspection, check the date, because roots and damage grow over time.

After repeated clogs: finding the real cause

.

If a line clogs every few months, the clog is a symptom. Roots, a sag, a bad joint or heavy scale is usually the cause, and cleaning without a look only resets the clock.

A camera pass after cleaning shows what is inside and where. That points the next step toward hydro jetting for buildup or toward sewer line repair for a damaged pipe.

Locating a line and finding a cleanout

.

Camera locators help when you do not know where the sewer runs. Many 1960s tract homes have unmarked laterals, and a locator maps the path before anyone digs for a patio, fence or addition.

Knowing the line's route and depth also helps if you plan a pool, a block wall or a large tree. Planting over a known sewer path is a common cause of future root trouble.

Who is responsible for what the camera finds

.

Midway City Sanitary District serves all of Westminster and Midway City, with its office at 14451 Cedarwood St and a phone at (714) 893-3553. Where property line responsibility starts is something to confirm with the district or a plumber.

If the camera shows a problem near the street connection, bring the footage to the district. It gives them a specific location and condition to discuss, which is more useful than a general description. If the footage shows a blockage rather than damage, sewer line cleaning is the next step, and the same visit can often lead into it.

Sewer smell inside the house: a quick check first

.

A sewer smell from one drain often means a dry p-trap, not a broken line. Run water in that drain for a minute, especially in guest baths or floor drains that rarely get used.

If the smell persists after the trap is refilled, or it appears in several rooms, a camera pass can check the line and vent connections. A faint smell near a laundry standpipe is worth a look too.

Standards and local sources.

Sewer camera inspection questions from Westminster homeowners

.
Do you offer sewer camera inspections when there is an urgent backup?

Our phone line is answered 24/7, including nights, weekends and holidays. If a line is backed up, we clear it first and then run the camera to find the cause.

How long does a sewer camera inspection take?

A typical inspection takes under an hour when there is a cleanout. Longer lines, tight bends or the need to clear debris first can add time.

Should I get a camera inspection before buying a house?

It is a good idea, especially for homes from the 1960s and 1970s. It shows the lateral's condition so you can plan for repairs before you close.

Will I get a recording of the inspection?

Ask for the footage and a written summary when you call, so you have a record of what the pipe looked like on the day. A recording helps if you need to compare quotes or discuss the line with the district.

Can the camera find where my sewer line runs?

Yes. A camera with a locator can mark the line's path and depth at the surface, which helps before digging for any yard project.

What does a camera inspection cost?

It depends on access, length and whether cleaning is needed first. We give a clear price before work starts, so you know what to expect.

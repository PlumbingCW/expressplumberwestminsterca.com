Before We Arrive: if you have a burst pipe or active leak

.

A few minutes of prep limit damage and let the plumber start work right away. Walk through these four steps in order.

1..Shut off the main valve

.

Turn the main water valve clockwise until it stops. If you cannot find it, call Garden Grove Water Services at (714) 741-5395, because the meter valve is usually city property.

2..Kill power near the water

.

Switch off breakers for wet rooms only if the panel is dry and you do not have to cross water. Never step into standing water near outlets or appliances.

3..Clear the area

.

Move rugs, boxes and furniture out of the wet zone, and lift valuables off the floor.

How Garden Grove water and sewer affect your plumbing

.

Garden Grove pumps about a dozen wells and blends in imported Metropolitan Water District supply. Groundwater averages about 313 ppm hardness, roughly 18 grains per gallon, while imported water runs near 160 ppm.

Harder groundwater means faster scale on water heater elements and tankless heat exchangers. For water questions, Garden Grove Water Services is at (714) 741-5395.

The city's Public Works sanitation crew maintains the public sewer, with more than 300 miles of line and 9,700 manholes. Property owners maintain their own laterals, which is why a backup in your home still needs a plumber. Public Works can be reached at (714) 741-5375.

Backup in the street or only at home?.

If a manhole is overflowing outside, call Public Works. If only your fixtures are backing up, the clog is often in your lateral, and our sewer line cleaning team can clear it.

Homes on former farmland, plumbing from the 1950s and 60s

.

Much of Garden Grove turned from farmland into single-family tracts during the 1950s and 1960s, and its sanitary district dates to 1924. Homes of that age may have galvanized supply lines or clay or cast iron drains, which are now past 50 years old.

Those materials corrode and crack. When a bathroom floods or a line backs up in an older home, a camera look through sewer camera inspection shows whether a spot repair or a bigger fix is needed.

How to pick the right service when you are not sure

.

Start with what you can see, hear or smell. A gurgling drain, a wet ceiling and a cold shower each point to a different page above, and the symptom tables on those pages narrow it down.

If two services seem to fit, call anyway. A plumber can sort out the cause over the phone and send the right person with the right parts.

What every service call includes

.

Each visit starts with a look at the problem and a clear explanation of the fix. You hear the price before work begins, and you decide whether to go ahead.

Our work area is Westminster, Midway City, Garden Grove, Fountain Valley and Stanton. See the areas we serve page, or the page for Garden Grove, for more on coverage.

Standards and local sources.

Plumbing work in California follows the California Plumbing Code (Title 24, Part 5), and some jobs, such as water heater changes, may need a permit. Ask the City of Westminster Building Division at (714) 548-3254 before you start.

Questions about our plumbing services

.
Which services are available as emergency calls?

Leak, drain, sewer, water heater, burst pipe and fixture failures all qualify. Planned installs and upgrades can be scheduled, though same-day visits are offered when available.

Do you work on commercial properties?

Yes. Shops, restaurants and offices in the area can call for leaks, drain problems and backflow questions. See the commercial plumbing page for details.

Can I get a plumber the same day?

Same-day appointments are offered when available. Call early and describe the problem so the right person is sent.

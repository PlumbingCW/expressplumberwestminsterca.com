Privacy Policy

.

What we collect, why, and the choices you have.

This policy explains how Westminster Express Plumbing handles information when you visit this website or call our phone line. It was last updated on October 24, 2026.

Information we collect

.

This website has no forms, accounts or comment sections, so we do not collect information through it directly. Our hosting provider may keep standard technical logs, such as IP address, browser type and pages requested.

When you call us, we collect the details you share, such as your name, address, phone number and the plumbing problem. Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes.

How we use information

.

We use call information to understand the problem, schedule service, provide a price and follow up on the work. We may also use recordings to train staff and check the quality of the advice given.

We do not sell your personal information.

Sharing

.

We share information only when needed to do the work or when the law requires it. That can include payment processors, parts suppliers and government agencies that issue permits.

Cookies and analytics

.

This site does not set advertising cookies. If analytics tools are added in the future, this page will be updated to say so.

Your choices

.

You can ask us what information we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing contact@expressplumberwestminsterca.com. If you do not want a call recorded, tell the person who answers at the start of the call.

.

Emergency Hydro Jetting in Westminster, CA

.

Hydro jetting uses high-pressure water to scour a pipe clean when a cable only punches a hole through the clog.

Call (714) 750-8637.See all services.

Our phone line is open 24/7 if a line is backing up now. While you wait, stop using water in the house, since every flush or load of laundry adds to a line that cannot drain.

Hydro jetting sends a controlled stream of water through the pipe to cut grease, scale and root growth off the walls. It is the right choice when a line clogs again and again, and the wrong choice for some failing pipes. We help sort out which one you have.

Emergency backup: stop the water before you jet anything

.

A line that is backing up cannot be cleaned until it is safe to work on. Stop water use, keep people away from the lowest fixtures and clear floors of anything that wicks moisture.

If sewage is reaching living space, keep children and pets out and wash hands after contact. Jetting is often the follow-up to a cable that cleared the clog but left the pipe coated.

What hydro jetting does that a cable cannot

.

A cable cuts a hole through a clog. The grease or scale on the pipe wall stays, and the hole fills in again, sometimes within weeks.

A jetting nozzle fires water backward and forward while it moves down the line, scouring the entire inside of the pipe. It is the closest thing to resetting a line back to its original diameter without replacing it.

When a camera should go in first

.

Jetting at pressure is not right for every pipe. A cracked, collapsed or badly offset line can be damaged or can fail to clear, and it is better to know that first.

A camera inspection shows the pipe condition, the clog type and where the line bends. We recommend one before jetting a line that has never been looked at, or one that keeps clogging for no obvious reason.

Which pipes suit jetting and which do not

.

Cast iron, clay and plastic lines in sound condition handle jetting well. Westminster's 1960s and 1970s tracts often have clay or cast iron laterals, and those can be tough on the surface but weak at the joints.

A pipe that is intact but rough with scale is a good candidate. A pipe with broken joints, offsets or a belly full of standing water needs repair first, and jetting would only wash debris into the gap.

Pressure is set to the pipe.

Jetting pressure and nozzle choice are matched to the pipe size and material. A fragile old line gets a gentler pass than a thick modern one.

What affects the cost of a jetting job

.

Jetting costs more than a basic cable job and often runs from a few hundred dollars to over a thousand, depending on the job. These are general industry ranges, not our price, and we give you a clear price before work starts.

The main drivers are line length, access point, how heavy the buildup is and whether a camera look is added. A clean access point such as a cleanout makes the job faster and cheaper than pulling a toilet.

Restaurants, apartments and shared lines

.

Shops and restaurants along Bolsa Avenue and Westminster Boulevard put steady grease through their lines. Scheduled jetting can keep those lines open between problems, and it helps avoid a Friday night shutdown.

Multi-unit buildings share a main, so one tenant's grease becomes everyone's backup. If your building has repeated issues, note which units are affected when you call, and see commercial plumbing for business lines.

Hydro jetting questions from Westminster property owners

.
Do you handle hydro jetting emergencies at night or on weekends in Westminster?

Yes. The phone line is answered 24/7, including nights, weekends and holidays. Call and describe the backup, and we will explain the next steps.

Is hydro jetting safe for old pipes?

It can be, if the pipe is sound. A camera look first shows cracks, offsets or collapse. If the pipe is fragile, we recommend repair before jetting.

How is jetting different from snaking?

A cable cuts a path through a clog. Jetting scours the whole pipe wall with water, so it removes grease and scale that a cable leaves behind.

Will jetting remove tree roots?

It cuts and flushes smaller roots well. Roots can regrow if they still enter at a joint or crack, so a repair may be needed for a lasting fix.

How often should a restaurant line be jetted?

It depends on the menu and volume. Heavy fryer use calls for more frequent service. We can suggest a schedule after seeing how quickly the line fills.

Does jetting cost more than regular drain cleaning?

Usually yes, since it needs larger equipment and more time. It also lasts longer when grease or scale is the problem, so repeat visits drop.

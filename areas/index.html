Westminster Express Plumbing is based on Goldenwest Street and serves the city plus the communities around it. This page explains where we work and how city boundaries affect who you call for a water or sewer problem.

Our emergency line is open 24/7 for every area on this page. Call with your address and we will confirm service for your block.

Westminster and Midway City

.

Westminster has about 91,000 residents and three ZIP codes: 92683, 92684 and 92685. Its neighbors are Garden Grove to the north and east, Seal Beach to the west, and Huntington Beach and Fountain Valley to the south.

Midway City is an unincorporated pocket that withdrew when Westminster incorporated in 1957. Sewer service for both comes from the Midway City Sanitary District, whose office is at (714) 893-3553.

Plumbing emergency in the area? Our phone line is open 24/7.

Call (714) 750-8637.

Garden Grove has its own page

.

Garden Grove is the one neighboring city with a dedicated page. It covers local water, sewer, neighborhoods and ZIP codes, so see Garden Grove emergency plumbing for details.

Garden Grove's ZIP codes run from 92840 to 92846, and we list the four nearest Westminster.

Fountain Valley and Stanton

.

Fountain Valley lies south of Westminster and Stanton sits between Westminster and west Garden Grove. We take calls from both, and the 24/7 line works the same way for them as for Westminster addresses.

Neither city has a separate page on this site. Call with your address to confirm availability.

Standards and local sources.

According to the Midway City Sanitary District, it serves all of Westminster and Midway City for sewer service. Water in Westminster is supplied by the city, and the City of Westminster's 2024 Water Quality Report lists nine groundwater wells plus three imported connections.

Business plumbing emergency: what to do right now

.

A burst line, a flooding restroom or a floor drain overflowing in a kitchen all call for the same first move: stop the water. Find the fixture shutoff, the suite shutoff or the building main.

If sewage is involved, keep customers and staff out of the area and do not run more water. If you smell gas, leave the building and call SoCalGas at 1-800-427-2200 from outside if SoCalGas is your gas provider.

Tenant, landlord and the shutoff question

.

In a shared building, the first problem is often who can reach the valve. Suite shutoffs may sit in a utility closet, above a ceiling tile or in the landlord's mechanical room.

Ask your property manager now where the suite and building shutoffs are, and who holds the key. After hours, a locked room can add delay. If a lease says who pays for which pipes, read it, because we cannot decide that for you.

Hidden leaks in commercial buildings

.

Commercial leaks hide behind wall panels, above ceilings and under slab floors. A water bill that creeps upward or a meter that moves after closing time is the first clue.

We pressure test, listen and use thermal tools to find the source before opening anything, the same method described on our leak detection page. In multi-tenant buildings, we also help work out whether the leak is in your suite or in a shared line.

Backflow testing and why businesses need it

.

A backflow device prevents dirty water from flowing backward into the drinking supply. Businesses with irrigation, fire lines, boilers or food service often have one.

Testing is usually done by a certified tester on a schedule set by the water provider. Ask the City Water Division at (714) 894-3796 what applies to you, and ask the Building Division at (714) 548-3254 whether a permit applies to your device. We do not list backflow testing as a service on this site.

Keep records handy.

Hold on to test tags and reports for any backflow device. If your device fails a test, repair or replacement usually has to happen before the water provider will accept the result.

Hard water and wear on commercial equipment

.

Westminster water averages 252 mg/L hardness, roughly 14.7 grains per gallon, and ranges from 138 to 413 across the system. Scale forms on water heaters, coffee and ice equipment, dishwashers and faucet aerators.

That scale cuts efficiency and shortens equipment life. Ask us about softening and filtration for kitchens and salons, and plan regular flushing of heaters.

Grease traps, lift pumps and what we do not do

.

Grease trap pumping is a separate trade, usually handled by a licensed hauler. We can look at the plumbing around the trap, but we do not pump it.

Sewage ejector and lift pumps are another service point in below-grade restrooms. If a pump alarm sounds, stop using the fixtures that feed it and call. Restaurants should ask their county health inspector whether a closure or notice is needed after a sewage spill, since rules vary.

Planning a commercial plumbing visit

.

A little preparation saves time. Give us access to the suite, shutoffs and any ceiling panels, and tell us about previous repairs.

We give you a clear price before work starts. Tell us your opening hours when you call, and urgent failures go through our 24/7 phone line.

Standards and local sources.

Commercial plumbing follows the California Plumbing Code (Title 24, Part 5), and the City Building Division, (714) 548-3254, can say whether a permit applies to your space. Verify any contractor's license on the Contractors State License Board website.

Commercial plumbing questions from Westminster businesses

.
Do you handle commercial plumbing emergencies at night or on weekends in Westminster?

Yes. The phone line is answered 24/7, including nights, weekends and holidays. Shut off the nearest valve, then call and tell us what the building is doing.

Who pays for a plumbing repair in my leased suite?

That depends on your lease and the location of the failure. Check the lease and talk to your landlord. We can describe what failed and where.

Can you find a leak in a multi-tenant building?

Yes. We test and listen to isolate whether the leak is in your suite or in a shared line, then explain what we found.

Do you pump grease traps?

No. Grease trap pumping is a separate trade. We can repair the plumbing connected to the trap.

Does my business need backflow testing?

Many do. Ask the City Building Division at (714) 548-3254 whether a test or permit applies to your device.

What should a restaurant do after a sewage backup?

Stop service in the affected area, keep staff away and call us. Contact your county health inspector to ask what notice or closure rules apply.
